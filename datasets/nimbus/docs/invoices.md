Nimbus issues one invoice per organization on the 1st of each month (UTC). It covers the seats for the month ahead and the usage of the month that just ended: extra requests, bandwidth, build minutes and storage above what your plan includes. Invoices are numbered like `NMB-2026-004512` and can be downloaded as PDF from Console > Billing > Invoices.

You can add a tax ID, a purchase order number and up to five billing email addresses. These details only apply to invoices issued after the change. An issued invoice cannot be edited, but you can ask support for a corrected copy within 30 days of the issue date.
