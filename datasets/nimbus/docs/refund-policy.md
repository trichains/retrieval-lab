lang: en
tags: [billing]
---

Monthly plans are not refundable. Annual plans can be refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the purchase; after that, there are no prorated refunds. If you downgrade or remove members in the middle of a cycle, the unused value becomes account credit on your next invoice, not money back.

Usage charges are only refunded when they come from a billing error on our side, such as a duplicate charge. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days. To ask for one, open a support ticket with the invoice number.
